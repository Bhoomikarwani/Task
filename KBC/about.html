About the Show

The Format

Contestants answer a series of multiple-choice questions to win large cash prizes up to 7 Crore.

Lifelines

Stuck on a question ? Use 50:50 , Auidence Poll or Video Call a friend to survive.

The Host

Hosted by the legendary Mr. Amitabh Bachchan , guiding you through the toughest questions.

Milestones in KBC History

  • 2000 (The Launch): KBC premieres on Star Plus on July 3rd. It is the first time a Bollywood superstar (Amitabh Bachchan) hosts a TV show. The top prize is ₹1 Crore.
  • 2001 (First Winner): Harshvardhan Nawathe becomes the first-ever contestant to win the ₹1 Crore jackpot.
  • 2005 (The Return): After a 4-year hiatus, the show returns as KBC Dwitiya with the prize money doubled to ₹2 Crores.
  • 2007 (The King Khan Era): Season 3 is hosted by Shah Rukh Khan.
  • 2011 (₹5 Crore Jackpot): Sushil Kumar, a computer operator from Bihar, creates history by winning the newly increased jackpot of ₹5 Crores.
  • 2013 (Top Prize Increases): For Season 7, the maximum prize is raised to ₹7 Crores.
  • 2020 (Digital Revolution): Due to the pandemic, Season 12 introduces digital selection processes and removes the "Audience Poll" lifeline for the first time.
  • 2022 (75 Years of Independence): To celebrate India's independence, the jackpot is temporarily raised to ₹7.5 Crores.

Game Rules and Format

  • Fastest Finger First (FFF)

    Before reaching the Hot Seat, contestants must pass the elimination round.

    • The Challenge: All 10 contestants are asked one question where they must arrange four options in the correct order (chronological, alphabetical, etc.).
    • The Winner: The contestant who answers correctly in the shortest amount of time wins the chance to sit on the Hot Seat opposite the host.
  • The Main Gameplay

    Once on the Hot Seat, the game is a test of knowledge, nerve, and strategy.

    • The Ladder: The contestant must answer a series of multiple-choice questions (usually 15 to 17 levels).
    • Prize Money: Each question carries a specific cash prize. As the level increases, so does the difficulty and the money.
    • The Timer:
      • Tier 1 (Early Questions): Usually has a 30-45 second timer.
      • Tier 2 (Middle Questions): Usually has a 60-second timer.
      • Tier 3 (High Stakes): The clock stops; there is no time limit for the final difficult questions.
  • The Safety Nets (Padav)

    To secure their winnings, the game has "Safety Nets" (fixed milestones).

    • If a contestant answers incorrectly, their winnings drop down to the last crossed Safety Net amount, not zero.
    • Common Safety Nets: ₹10,000, ₹3,20,000, and ₹75,00,000 (amounts vary by season).
